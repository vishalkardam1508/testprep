## 🤖 Vedha Tutor: create your own AI test, free

Month-wise and field-wise **GS & Current Affairs** tests built around your exam's real pattern, not a generic question dump.

### How it works

**1. Tell us your exam 📝**
Enter the exam or job title and paste the notification. Pick the **year**, **months** and **fields**, and choose **English or Hindi**.

**2. Vedha Tutor studies the pattern 🧠**
It reads the last 5 years of papers, works out the sections, difficulty, timing and marking, and searches the web for verified current affairs.

**3. Practise 25–100 questions 🎯**
Start after the first 5 questions while Vedha Tutor writes the rest. Take a **timed exam** with a full result, or use **Learn mode** to see answers and explanations instantly.

### Fields you can choose

`Economy` · `Polity` · `International` · `Science & Tech` · `Sports` · `Awards` · `Schemes` · `History` · `Geography`

> **Free · No registration · Ready in about a minute**

## 🚀 Getting started

1. Open **[gov-tayari.vercel.app](https://gov-tayari.vercel.app/)**.
2. Choose your exam and start a free mock test, **or**
3. Click **Create AI Test** to build a custom GS / current affairs test, **or**
4. Click **Add Test** to practise your own questions.
5. Check **My Results** to track your performance.
